# Privacy Policy: 逼落車 Exit Rush

**Effective date:** 2026-10-13  
**Developer:** Kingsley Kwan, Hong Kong · Contact: kingsleykwan0705@icloud.com

## English

**Summary:** Exit Rush does not create accounts or collect contact information. Game progress stays on your device. Optional ads (Google AdMob, non-personalized) and optional in-app purchases (Apple) may involve limited data as described below. We do not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) for tracking across apps or websites.

1. **What the app itself stores.** Your game progress and settings (levels cleared, skill points, language, graphics quality, volume, purchase unlocks mirrored locally) are saved **only on your device**. We do not operate a backend that receives this save data.

2. **No accounts.** There is no sign-in, no profile, and no contact list collected by the app.

3. **Advertising (Google AdMob).** The iOS build may show optional interstitial ads after certain first-time level clears. Ads use **non-personalized** requests (`npa`). We do **not** show an ATT prompt and do **not** track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as:
   - Device identifiers (for example an app- or developer-scoped device ID; not IDFA tracking)
   - Advertising data (for example ads shown)
   - Product interaction (for example ad interactions)
   - Diagnostics / performance data related to the ads SDK
   - IP address (which may be used to estimate coarse location)
   
   Google may use this data to deliver and measure ads (third-party advertising) and to improve its SDK. See Google’s policies:  
   <https://policies.google.com/privacy> · AdMob disclosure guide: <https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure>

4. **In-app purchases (Apple).** Optional non-consumable purchases (character unlocks, Remove Ads) are processed by Apple. Apple may associate **purchase history** with your Apple ID for App Store commerce. Restore Purchases is available in Settings. See Apple’s privacy policy: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>.

5. **Remove Ads.** Buying the Remove Ads product (or a paid character, per in-app rules) disables interstitial ads. Then AdMob is not used for those impressions.

6. **Network.** Core gameplay runs offline. Network access is used for ads (when shown) and for StoreKit purchase/restore. The separate web version on GitHub Pages may involve host logs (e.g. IP); see GitHub’s privacy statement: <https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement>.

7. **Children.** We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond what the third-party SDKs above may process as described.

8. **Deleting your data.** Delete the app to remove on-device saves. For Google/Apple data, use their respective account and privacy tools.

9. **Tracking.** We do not track you across apps or websites. The app does not use ATT / IDFA for tracking.

10. **Changes.** If practices change, we will update this policy and App Store Connect App Privacy labels, and revise the effective date above.

11. **Contact.** kingsleykwan0705@icloud.com

*Hong City Rail / 香城鐵路 is fictional. This game is a parody and is not affiliated with any real railway operator.*

## 繁體中文

**摘要：**《逼落車 Exit Rush》唔會開帳戶、亦唔會收集聯絡資料。遊戲進度只儲喺你部裝置。可選廣告（Google AdMob，非個人化）同可選 App 內購買（經 Apple）可能涉及下列有限資料。我哋唔會要求「App 追蹤透明度」(ATT)，亦唔會用廣告識別碼（IDFA）做跨 App／網站追蹤。

1. **App 本身儲存嘅資料：** 遊戲進度同設定（過關、技能點、語言、畫質、音量、本機解鎖狀態）**只會儲存喺你部裝置**；我哋冇後端接收呢啲存檔。

2. **冇帳戶：** 冇登入、冇個人檔案、App 本身唔收集聯絡人。

3. **廣告（Google AdMob）：** iOS 版可能喺部分首次過關後顯示插頁廣告，並使用**非個人化**請求（`npa`）。我哋**唔會**顯示 ATT 提示，亦**唔會**跨其他公司嘅 App 或網站追蹤你。Google Mobile Ads SDK 可能收集例如：裝置識別碼、廣告資料、產品互動、與廣告 SDK 相關嘅診斷／效能資料、IP 地址（可能用以估計粗略位置）。Google 可能用呢啲資料投放同衡量廣告，以及改進 SDK。詳見 Google 私隱政策同 AdMob 披露指引（連結見英文版）。

4. **App 內購買（Apple）：** 角色解鎖、去除廣告等非消耗型項目由 Apple 處理；Apple 可能將**購買記錄**同你嘅 Apple ID 關聯。設定內提供「還原購買」。詳見 Apple 私隱政策。

5. **去除廣告：** 購買去除廣告（或按遊戲規則購買付費角色）後會停用插頁廣告。

6. **網絡：** 核心玩法可離線。顯示廣告或進行／還原購買時會用到網絡。網頁版由 GitHub Pages 提供，寄存方可記錄 IP 等技術資料。

7. **兒童：** 我哋唔會蓄意收集 13 歲以下兒童嘅個人資料（除上述第三方 SDK 按各自政策處理嘅範圍）。

8. **刪除資料：** 刪除 App 可清走本機存檔；Google／Apple 資料請用佢哋嘅帳戶同私隱工具處理。

9. **追蹤：** 我哋唔會跨 App 或網站追蹤你，亦唔會用 ATT／IDFA 做追蹤。

10. **修改：** 如做法有變，我哋會更新本政策同 App Store Connect「App 私隱」標籤，並更新生效日期。

11. **聯絡：** kingsleykwan0705@icloud.com
